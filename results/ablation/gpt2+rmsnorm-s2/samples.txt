
And to the tainture of this day my dear
Hath sought the first; and that, I would have it,
Should have been as to be so well.

POSTHUMUS.
I know not.

IACHIMO.
Who’s my father?

POSTHUMUS.
I have said.

IACHIMO.
For any man nor no man’s sake,
But not so much as he does, but yet ’tis well;
And therefore ’tis not I know no more.

CYMBELINE.
I know
No more than you can, not that which I have
Was to be a child. I am not that I am yours
That I did do me wrong, or I say
I have been a thing more. What, my lord? Posthumus?

POSTHUMUS.
I think this island in your presence,
For that you are both in this.

IMOGEN.
A mother hath not fettern’d off
That would be my lord.

IACHIMO.
No?

IMOGEN.
You have done’t.

IMOGEN.
It is strange, madam.

IACHIMO.
I must be so far more of it;
For it that I may be my brother.

PISANIO.
Is there no time enough my father?

IACHIMO.
Is’t not a fool?

IMOGEN.
Ay, none, lady.

IACHIMO.
I am sorry that I have heard you.

CYMBELINE.
Why, I know not. Was it, I think it is.

IACHIMO.
My lord, I am ignorant, and my lord,
Your son?

IMOGEN.
Dear my ring, my lord, you are too good.

IMOGEN.
I did think me.

IACHIMO.
Not know’t, my lord
