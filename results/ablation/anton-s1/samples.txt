Is a nobleman to my house.

ANTIPHOLUS OF EPHESUS.
Good night, good night, good night.

ANGELO.
And what’s this?

ANGELO.
Here, Roderigo, to the Turk.

ANTIPHOLUS OF EPHESUS.
Good night, good Antonio; I know not what I am.
I have done much pains, good Antonio;
Nor did I know you thereof you are.

DROMIO OF SYRACUSE.
No, nor you can, sir, nor I’ll use you,
When you would know you in this place at all.

ANTIPHOLUS OF EPHESUS.
Pardon, sir, do you like yourself.

ANTIPHOLUS OF EPHESUS.
I will do thee well, lest I have done all.
I am sure you have been no more of mine.

ANTIPHOLUS OF EPHESUS.
A thousand thanks and your good thoughts,
But since you shall hear you do, sir,
By your patience and by you,
Let me have you well.

ANTIPHOLUS OF EPHESUS.
Let me obey.

 [_Exeunt._]

SCENE II. Forence. A room in the Castle.

 Enter Countess and Tooer.

ANGELO.
Now come to me here tonight.
I will bring you to your wife,
And see I’ll be sworn to bid her speak,
And leave her husband presently.

ANGELO.
I love her, sir, I pray you.

ANGELO.
Sir, I’ll bring her to you.

ANGELO.
I have her. Here comes the ring.

ANGELO.
I pray you, sir,
To have some of you.

ANGELO.
I am not.

DROMIO OF EPHESUS.
Come, come, come on.
