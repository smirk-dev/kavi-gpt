
WORCESTER.
My Lord of Westmoreland, the Duke of Norfolk,
Came on the Duke of Hastings Murderers,
The Duke of York and Westmoreland, with his head.

WESTMORELAND.
Silence, I say, to France, and all their lands.

HASTINGS.
Why stay we to our purpose? What is this?

GLOUCESTER.
A hundred sum of all days in England’s charge?

WESTMORELAND.
A gracious Duke of York, a king of York.

YORK.
’Tis no less than he is dead. It is not yet.

YORK.
But York, my lord, he is dead, my lord.

SUFFOLK.
And why should he be banished?

YORK.
An if it be, my sovereign, I did think
I was not the worst of the Duke alive.

YORK.
If Warwick be not proud, he’ll be his heir.

YORK.
I cannot tell him, as I did amend.

QUEEN MARGARET.
O Warwick, what art thou to thy father’s death?

WARWICK.
Ay, Warwick, there bethought his father’s king.

YORK.
What says Derby?

YORK.
Why, now, my lord, for I had thought so ill.

YORK.
It seems to be a subject to my lord.

YORK.
Then, Warwick, and thy father’s death is not
No sin of all that you would have done this.

WARWICK.
O, let me say that Edward is my wife.

KING LEWIS.
What, is this done with you?

KING HENRY.
Ay, not a while.
