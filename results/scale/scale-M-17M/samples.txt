
I have a pipe so far from all these hours
As to make me thankful.

DIOMEDES.
You are too far
Of this great lady’s name.

PROTEUS.
How?

DIOMEDES.
I’ll make her amends.

PROTEUS.
Go, get you gone.

DIOMEDES.
Here dwell you, madam; have you been at supper?

PROTEUS.
Why, madam, did you so? There’s a merry night,
That you must leave me.

PROTEUS.
Do I know that?

PROTEUS.
I am not well enough.

PROTEUS.
Do you hear this?

PROTEUS.
No, my lord.

PROTEUS.
No, no, ’tis very true.

PROTEUS.
Ay, and the next are well.

PROTEUS.
Why, madam, speak with me; I am sick
Of my own sickness.

PROTEUS.
I am sure I have not
So fit a reason for myself to think me cured.

PROTEUS.
I am asham’d for them; if not, my lord.

PROTEUS.
I would you had been so. I have been with you,
And been a gentleman, and yet have been
Too sadly wish’d, you had forgot myself
That you have been so foolish.

PROTEUS.
Most true, my lord.

PROTEUS.
I never took it of your brother’s house.
