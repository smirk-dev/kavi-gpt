
And to the sceptre of all my forehead
Upon a little messenger.

CERIMON.
O blessed heavens,
If I have power to do it so much!

PERICLES.
Where’s the matter?

CERIMON.
I’ll do’t, sir, let’s see ’t not.

MARINA.
We’ll bear it as your highness.

IMONIDES.
I thank your lordship.

PERICLES.
[_Aside._] Let me be sure, sir, if you please
To see one or any of them,
And she will never come to me.

PERICLES.
It is the next, sir, with a most good grace
That ever I give the king’s gift to me
Than she shall have spoke.

PERICLES.
Well, well,
You see the world or man of my life.
The lady, as well as you have said!

PERICLES.
This is the more my lord.

PERICLES.
I have, sir.

PERICLES.
The reason is, so: you are bound.

 Enter Dolabella, attended.

PERICLES.
I know it more. I have done in this,
A woman that I may give us thanks.

 Enter Gratiano and Marina.

PERICLES.
What’s the matter?

PERICLES.
That thou hast lost thyself, a son of father,
Of thy great self-serving?

PERICLES.
Here, sir,
Who hast thou but been, that thou dost make
A daughter to thyself and I will make a daughter.

PERICLES.
Too that, thou art believ’d; and have a soul
To be a Roman, who may be so long,
If thou wouldst hear the deed.
